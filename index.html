In brief

The evening before the stage

On the night before DDX’26 San Diego, Appsfactory hosted fourteen design, product and AI leaders from Apple, Mastercard, Sony, Adobe, Netflix, Target, Qualcomm, Dexcom, Oracle, Ancestry, Travelers, UC San Diego Health and American Specialty Health at one long table at Nolita Hall. No stage, no slides, no name badges.

DDX curates the table. For every city we invite a short list of the most senior decision-makers in design, product and innovation, a mix of DDX speakers and the enterprise leaders who rarely appear on a conference stage. Appsfactory, with its design studio Edenspiekermann, hosted the evening and set the theme; at every seat a card read “Your culture eats AI for breakfast.” The conversation ran from the first drink to the last espresso, and every guest was in the room again at 9 AM the next morning when the conference opened.

That is the point of the format: three hours of unscripted exchange with the people who sign off on the next design organisation, the next AI feature and the next agency partner, in a setting that no booth or sponsored talk can replace. This page documents the San Diego edition so that the next host knows exactly what to expect.

How it worksThe DDX dinner format

Curated by DDX, hosted by Appsfactory

01 · The list

DDX curates, the host approves

DDX built a priority list of senior design, product and innovation leaders in San Diego: enterprise decision-makers first, DDX speakers second. Invitations went out in waves and Appsfactory saw every confirmation.

02 · The evening

One table, Appsfactory’s theme

An invitation-only dinner the night before the conference, fourteen seats, a venue chosen for conversation rather than show. Appsfactory opened with its theme and a short welcome; the rest was unscripted.

03 · The morning after

The conversation continues on stage

Every guest was back at DDX the next day: at Appsfactory’s talk, in the table talks and at the happy hour. Photos, the guest list and this recap are part of the package for every host.
